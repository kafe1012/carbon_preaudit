## 4. Project Description and Expected Removals

The project is an afforestation and reforestation (ARR) activity on degraded former pasture in
Costa Rica. The project area covers 100.0 ha, delineated by the polygon provided in Annex A. The
activity follows the methodology VM0047 (Afforestation, Reforestation and Revegetation) under the
Verra VCS programme.

Aboveground biomass was estimated from three permanent sample plots of 400 m² each, using the
allometric equation of Chave et al. (2014). Plot values were scaled to the project area on an area
weighted basis.

The gross removals of the project amount to 8,364 tCO2e over the crediting period. A share of 20 %
of the gross removals is withheld in the buffer pool to cover the risk of reversal. After the buffer
deduction, the net removals available for issuance amount to 6,691 tCO2e.

Monitoring follows the plan in Section 9 and will be repeated every five years.
